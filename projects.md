## Project Icarus: competition rocket recovery system (in progress)
*Nottingham Space Society, entering UKSEDS NRC | Structures member*

I am responsible for the parachute recovery system and the payload bay. The design must meet fixed requirements: a descent speed below 15 m/s, recovery loads carried through the eyebolts, and a payload secured with three M3 bolts.

So far:
- Comparing parachute shapes and materials using a weighted decision matrix.
- Sizing the recovery system by hand calculation, verified in OpenRocket.
- Assessing how a hand-made canopy could fail ahead of planned ejection and load tests.
- Contributed recovery-methods research to the team's Preliminary Design Review.
